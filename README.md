# Laboratory vs Real-World Battery Operating Conditions

Research code and experimental results investigating differences
between laboratory battery cycling conditions and real-world
electric vehicle operating profiles.

## Research Objectives

- Compare laboratory and field operating domains.
- Analyse SOC, temperature and C-rate distributions.
- Examine battery stress and cycle-life relationships.
- Evaluate MIT charging-policy robustness.
- Investigate the representativeness of laboratory protocols.

## Research Datasets

- MIT: Battery cycling and charging-policy experiments
- KIT: Laboratory battery operating conditions
- TUM: Real-world EV operating profiles

Original datasets are not redistributed in this repository.

## Repository Contents

- src/ — Python research scripts
- paper_C_transfer/ — Figures, tables and experimental results

## Reproducibility

This is a research-code archive. End-to-end execution and
dataset provenance have not yet been independently verified.
